# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **MergePilot AI** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy developer community.

---

## Expected Behavior

As an open-source developer tool focused on code quality and pull request intelligence, we value rigor, clarity, and empathy. We expect participants to uphold the following standards:

- **Demonstrate Empathy and Kindness**: Treat every community member with respect and consideration, regardless of background or experience level.
- **Focus on Technical Merit**: Critique code, design choices, architectures, and algorithms—never people. Provide clear, constructive feedback supported by technical reasoning.
- **Welcome Newcomers**: Help onboarding developers, answer questions patiently, and guide first-time contributors with actionable suggestions.
- **Respect Differing Viewpoints**: Accept constructive criticism gracefully, listen actively to alternative engineering perspectives, and seek consensus.
- **Protect Community Trust**: Handle credentials, pull request data, and security matters responsibly in line with our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Graceful Collaboration**: Focus on what is best for the overall project, its maintainability, and its users rather than individual preferences.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited across all MergePilot AI project spaces:

- **Harassment and Discrimination**: Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, hate speech, and personal or political attacks.
- **Ad Hominem and Hostile Code Reviews**: Condescending, dismissive, or passive-aggressive language in review comments, issues, or pull request discussions.
- **Unwelcome Sexual Attention or Advances**: The use of sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome romantic or sexual advances, and inappropriate physical or digital contact.
- **Doxxing and Privacy Violations**: Publishing others' private information—such as physical addresses, phone numbers, private email addresses, or repository credentials—without their explicit, written consent.
- **Spamming and Promotional Abuse**: Using repository discussions, pull requests, or issues for unsolicited commercial advertising, spam, or search engine manipulation.
- **Bad Faith Participation**: Deliberately creating disruption, griefing the codebase, wasting community resources, or reopening settled discussions without new technical information.

---

## Technical Disagreements and Code Reviews

MergePilot AI analyzes pull requests and risk patterns, which naturally invites technical debate on architecture, rules, performance, and best practices.

When technical disagreements arise:

1. **Assume Good Intent**: Approach discussions with the assumption that your fellow contributors are working toward the same goal: making MergePilot AI reliable, fast, and secure.
2. **Back Arguments with Evidence**: Ground your feedback in profiling data, documentation, standard design patterns, type safety constraints, or benchmark results.
3. **Seek Common Ground**: Compromise where acceptable and escalate respectfully to maintainers when consensus cannot be reached.
4. **Accept Final Decisions**: Once maintainers evaluate the technical trade-offs and reach a decision, respect the outcome and move forward productively.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces associated with MergePilot AI, including:

- The GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit comments)
- Code review threads and documentation contributions
- Community communication channels, social media representations, or email threads involving the project
- When representing MergePilot AI in public spaces, technical talks, or conferences

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns regarding community conduct, please report it immediately to the project maintainers:

- **Contact**: `chavanparas0201@gmail.com`
- **Subject Line**: `[MergePilot AI] Code of Conduct Report`

### What to Include in a Report

To help us investigate and resolve the issue quickly, please provide:

1. Your contact information (so we can follow up with you).
2. The names, usernames, or handles of the individuals involved.
3. A description of the incident, including what occurred and when.
4. Links to relevant issues, pull requests, commit comments, or screenshots where applicable.
5. Any additional context that may help us understand the situation.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated with strict confidentiality. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest with a report will recuse themselves from the deliberation.

> [!NOTE]
> If you are reporting a technical vulnerability, secret exposure, or software exploit rather than a community conduct issue, please follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement Responsibilities and Guidelines

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers will follow these enforcement guidelines:

### 1. Correction

- **Impact**: Use of inappropriate language, unprofessional behavior, or minor boundary violations in project spaces.
- **Action**: A private, written warning from maintainers providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

- **Impact**: A violation through a single incident or an escalating pattern of inappropriate behavior.
- **Action**: A formal warning detailing the consequences of continued unacceptable behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

- **Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained disruptive behavior, harassment, or refusal to abide by maintainer decisions.
- **Action**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project spaces for a specified period of time.

### 4. Permanent Ban

- **Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained harassment of an individual, aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals, or intentional malicious disruption.
- **Action**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project repositories and community spaces.
